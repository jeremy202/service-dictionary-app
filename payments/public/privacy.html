Privacy Policy

Service Dictionary · Last updated 13 November 2026

Service Dictionary is an offline reference of service terms, abbreviations, prowords and symbols. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We keep it to the minimum needed to run the free trial and paid plans.

What we collect

Words you search for, entries you save and your recent list stay on your phone. They are never sent to us. The dictionary works without an internet connection.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Payments

In the Google Play version, payments are handled by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We never see your card or bank details. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions.

How long we keep it

The trial record (device identifier and start date) is kept so the trial cannot be repeated. You can ask us to delete it at any time; Premium purchases made through Google Play stay linked to your Google account and can be restored after deletion.

Security

All communication between the app and our payment service is encrypted (HTTPS).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and contact

To ask a question or request deletion of your data, use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
